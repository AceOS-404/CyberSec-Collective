## How the Red Team labs are documented

Every practical records its objective, authorised scope, environment, commands and explanations, actual observations, troubleshooting, limits and any redacted screenshots. Theory and planned tasks are kept distinct from completed exercises; no unperformed result is claimed.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Authorised, contained training environments only.** Do not use these exercises against other people's networks, live infrastructure or systems outside the approved scope. Never publish credentials, identifiable device information or unredacted evidence. Each person is responsible for their own actions.
