# Public repository safety and ethical scope

## Scope requirement
Every lab must list who authorised the work, whether the target is fictional/public/owned, and boundaries. Training captures and deliberately vulnerable VMs are preferable for public demonstrations.

## Before publishing
- Remove genuine passwords, authentication cookies, SSH keys, API tokens, client identities and private network details.
- Review every screenshot for browser tabs, desktop notifications, personal messages, exposed accounts, and credentials.
- Do not publish non-public packet captures or unredacted logs.
- Attribute external sample data, code and tutorials; do not republish paid instructor content.
- Keep examples limited to authorised testing and avoid running traffic against real targets.
- State what was observed, what was simulated, and what was not achieved.

## Disclaimer
This is an independent educational record, not an invitation or authorisation to test other people's systems. The material may not fit every environment and comes without a guarantee of safety. Use responsibly and lawfully.
